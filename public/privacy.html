Privacy

Last updated November 10, 2026

SeekMakeup is a directory of makeup brands and the stores that sell them. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it, in plain language.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you join the launch list. We also save the date and time you signed up.
  • What you send us through the contact form: your email, your message and, if you give it, your name.
  • Basic visit counts, such as which pages are viewed and roughly where visitors come from. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies and doesn't track you across other websites.

We don't ask for your name, phone number, location or payment details.

How we use it

  • To send you one email when SeekMakeup launches.
  • If you tell us you own a store, to contact you about a free listing.
  • If you send us a message through the contact form, to reply to it.
  • To understand how many people visit and which pages are useful.

We don't sell, rent or share your email address with brands, stores or advertisers.

Where it's stored

Your email and any messages are stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this site. Only SeekMakeup can see them.

Removing your email

Every email we send will include a way to unsubscribe. You can also ask us to delete your address at any time through our contact form (choose "Remove my email").

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
